Character roster

The Crew

Six people, one block, one purple diamond. Here's who's actually holding it down.

Leo

Lead presence

Frontline voice of the universe. Calm pressure. Street authority. Big-screen energy. Leo doesn't lead by volume — he leads by being the one person on the block everyone trusts to make the call when it actually matters. Talks slow, moves faster than anyone expects, and never repeats himself twice.

Rico

Style specialist

Smooth operator with charisma, timing, and enough swagger to annoy lesser men on sight. Rico can talk his way into any room and out of most trouble. He's the one who makes the crew look effortless, even when it isn't — the walking proof that presentation is its own kind of power.

Mustachio

Silent muscle

Precision, discipline, and danger. Speaks rarely. Means business every time. Mustachio is the one nobody wants to test twice — not because he's loud about it, but because he's never once needed to be. Loyalty runs deeper than words with him.

Bonny

Bold protector

Smart, fearless, and impossible to overlook when pressure hits the block. Bonny reads a room before anyone else does and acts on it without hesitation. She's the reason the crew stays two steps ahead instead of one step behind.

Quita

Quiet soldier

Low-key force with watchful energy and the kind of patience that wins ugly situations. Quita is easy to underestimate right up until the moment that's a mistake. She keeps the crew's secrets and its timing, both equally well.

A.R.F.

Guardian presence

Heavy chain, heavy loyalty, heavy consequences for anyone confusing kindness with weakness. A.R.F. is the crew's anchor — the one who shows up first and leaves last, every single time, no exceptions.
