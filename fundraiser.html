The Story

Why this exists

Hood Catz began as a creative project inspired by Chicago street culture, loyalty, and community — the block, the crew, the purple diamond. Somewhere along the way it became something bigger than a cast of characters.

This fundraiser NFT collection is built to help support programs that assist families and animals through difficult times, alongside the community work already underway through Northwestern Settlement House in Chicago. Every mint moves both the universe and the mission forward at the same time.

What the fundraiser supports

  • Programs for families facing hardship
  • Animal welfare and rescue efforts
  • Northwestern Settlement House community programs (Chicago)
  • Continued production of the Hood Catz universe
